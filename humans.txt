  CampusQuiet — how busy is your campus right now
  Illinois: https://campusquiet.com/
  Ohio State: https://campusquiet.com/osu

  A student project. Not affiliated with any university.

  Busyness is estimated from published class schedules and building hours,
  not measured by sensors. If the map says a building is closed and you are
  sitting in it, that is a real error and worth an email — corrections are
  the only thing that makes it better than the schedules it reads.
